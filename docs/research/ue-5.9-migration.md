## 1. Preview status, timing and rollback

**Fact:** Epic says this preview had roughly a week of development and shorter QA, is unstable, and should be tested on a copy. Its original announcement places the eventual full release in **2027**, without a firm date. It promised fixes during preview and a Mac/Linux Web QFE “this week”; that is a historical November 9 promise, not a current ETA. Preview 2 shipped November 19: **CL 60738873**, tag `5.9.0-preview-2`. No exact subsequent hotfix/full-release date was verified. [Epic announcement, posts 1/3; Preview 2 posts 41/42.]

**Known-issue relevance:** Preview 1 lists Python Blueprint root-swap crashes (UE-411502) and missing default toolsets (UE-412135). Preview 2 marks the toolset-default issue and missing Mac Wasm files fixed. Its open issues include source installed-build `-clean` deleting editor binaries (UE-415141) and cook failure with plugin `DirectoriesToNeverCook` paths through AllToolsets (UE-413659); also WidgetFinder, Slate hit visibility and MVVM binding removal bugs. This is not a complete engine defect inventory. [Same staff posts.]

**Fact:** Epic's upgrade guidance warns that upgraded projects do not open in older versions. Issue #276's supplied operator plan requires a `ue-5.8-final` tag and keeping 5.8 installed; local rules require CLI Git, local-file LFS backup and exclusive asset ownership. A tag protects references, not missing LFS object payloads. **Inference:** verify the rollback tag and its content are recoverable through the configured local LFS storage before any conversion save. Do not send these objects to GitHub LFS. [Epic upgrade guidance; supplied #276 body; `AGENTS.md:46–51`.]

**Plan correction needed at implementation:** “no saving until checks pass” cannot encompass a completed bake: `scripts/pcg/bake` invokes a saving World Partition builder. `editor.sh close` also explicitly saves dirty packages, and `restart` calls `close`. The lead should use a read-only smoke phase, then a deliberate conversion/bake phase on the migration branch with the rollback point secured. Do not use ordinary `close` or `restart` to discard a failed no-save trial. This does not require changing the operator's migration decision; it makes its phases executable. [`scripts/pcg/bake:8–12,50–54`; `scripts/editor.sh:197–212,263–264`.]
