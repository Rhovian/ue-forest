# Interior forest-floor terrain: diagnosis and ranked trials

Research, 2026-11-07. Proposed repository destination: `docs/research/forest-floor-terrain.md`. Read-only worker report; the lead must land it. No editor connection, project edit, build, gate, import or asset save was performed.

**The largest missing terrain feature is intermediate-scale relief: low moss cushions, shallow hollows and grounded root/log contacts between the island's broad slopes and its fine texture detail.** Our supplied interior already has green floor and fern colonies, but the exposed ground reads as one smooth textured sheet. Start with a bounded relief trial using installed mossy geometry and the existing floor/coating; consider Nanite Landscape displacement next. A whole terrain-system replacement is a much larger intervention. These are visual diagnosis and design recommendations, grounded in the images and current builders, not a measured performance result. [V1–V3; L1–L4]

**The Beech pack's presentation is not an included terrain recipe.** Quixel explicitly says rendered examples contain terrain materials, ground and extra scatter absent from the pack. Its preview demonstrates believable uneven support, debris and object contact, but the concept's almost continuous moss is the governing coverage target. Do not reproduce the preview's extensive brown litter. [F1; V3; L1:201–204]

## What the current pipeline can and cannot supply

- Terrain uses DGM1 source samples, reduced to **2 m vertex spacing**, with a 1513-square output over the 3024 m vertex span. Encoding is 16-bit. That preserves island-scale elevation but cannot express independent sub-metre cushions at arbitrary positions; raising height precision alone does not increase horizontal resolution. [L2:20–21,143–151; sampling inference]
- Floor defaults already include `ForestMoss=1`, moss at 4 m tile size, two-scale texture sampling, patch masks and macro variation. Outputs are base color, normal, AO and roughness; the builder connects no displacement or WPO. It is therefore wrong to diagnose this as “no moss system” or assume a global noise increase will add shape. Actual saved material-instance overrides remain unverified. [L3:24–60,167–194,205–220,259–267]
- Shared moss coating already samples the same moss surface and shared tint and outputs color, normalized blended normal, roughness and mask. Keep that previous research/implementation rather than inventing another bark system. Relief and adjacency are the new work. [L4:115–131; L8]
- Ground placement has candidate budgeting. Extending scatter over the whole biome can cost candidate-generation work even when final accepted counts look modest. Terrain import explicitly selects a Landscape and its verification traces reject other actors; some offline PCG sampling reads the saved height PNG. Geometry added above it needs a deliberate projection/contact policy. [L5:101–130; L6:58–70,88–102; L7:28–39]
- `Tileable` and `Decoration` in `/Game/EuropeanBeech` are names, not proof of forest-floor textures. Their material instances also occur under Forest/Field/Sapling/Seedling tree folders. The Asset Zoo map exists, but neither its graph nor texture content was inspected in the editor. Do not swap these into the landscape on filenames alone. [C1]

This report extends the three earlier studies: they already establish colony placement, assemblies, material continuity, selective relief, and the rejected Nordic Forest Ledge Moss. The new focus is terrain resolution, actual surface displacement and the UE 5.8 Mesh Terrain alternative. [L8; L1:174]

## Current UE5 options and project cost

Costs below are engineering estimates/inferences, **not timings**. Low/medium/high compare intervention scope and expected resource work in this project. No source establishes a universal fastest forest-floor recipe. [Scope: L2–L7; mechanisms: E1–E10]

| Technique | Capability and primary evidence | Cost and fit here |
|---|---|---|
| **Base heightmap / localized macro relief** | Landscape topology controls the support surface; components/sections carry rendering costs. [E1] | **Medium implementation risk if terrain changes; low extra shading cost at unchanged topology.** Useful for multi-metre swells/depressions. At our 2 m spacing, retain cushions in another layer. Halving spacing over the same area approaches 4× samples/quads, quartering it approaches 16× (grid calculation); upsampling alone adds no captured detail. Must reconcile shore/water masks, saved heights, tree grounding and regeneration. [L2,L6,L7; design inference] |
| **Material normals, height-aware blends, controlled texture scale** | Existing builder already supplies multi-scale B/N/ORM detail; the result alters shading without new shape. [L3] | **Low intervention**, but more samples/procedural noise add shader work. Existing loops request 3 layers × 3 texture types × 2 scales = 18 texture-sample nodes, before patch/noise; this is a graph count, not compiled fetch count. First inspect actual MI scale, normal response and texture content. Height used only as blend alpha does not displace anything. [L3:205–220,259–262; calculation/design inference] |
| **Opaque mesh ground pieces / scatter / assemblies** | PCG can preserve mesh/material/hierarchy data in reusable assemblies. [E2] Nanite supports instances, and finely layered or perforated geometry still needs profiling. [E3,E4] | **Medium setup; bounded geometry/streaming/shadow cost.** Directly supplies cushion silhouettes, embedded rock, roots and log contacts without replacing the island. Favor a few connected low forms over stacks of thin nearly coplanar sheets. Use installed suitable mossy boulders as the documented fallback; their stone silhouette may still fail the target. Do not promise soft moss from an unsuitable rock. [L1:174; C2; V1; design inference] |
| **Nanite Landscape alone** | Changes geometry representation, with identical source shape; retains both Nanite and ordinary Landscape data at runtime. [E5] | **Medium build/storage/memory intervention.** Can be a rendering optimization; enabling it alone will not create cushions. Actual Landscape flag/build state was not verified. [E5; L2; inference] |
| **Nanite runtime tessellation / displacement** | Experimental; creates triangles from a material height input. Needs enable CVars/material toggle; shading normal remains separate. Magnitude affects culling/VSM; seams may crack; pixel-size fade exists. [E4] | **Medium–high integration and GPU risk.** Strong candidate for continuous small relief, but start bounded and shallow. Must author/verify a true height field, tune local-space Landscape magnitude, keep normal/height registration, and verify collision/PCG contacts separately. Do not infer height from an arbitrary ORM channel. No verified Metal result or numerical budget here. [E4; L3,L6,L7; design inference] |
| **Offline displaced Nanite ground meshes** | Experimental static displacement bakes an optimized mesh before runtime. [E4] | **Medium authoring/build/storage**, avoids runtime tessellation for a static patch but adds mesh boundaries and placement management. Potential later solution for a reusable qualified ground form; not authorization to manufacture substitute moss assets contrary to the free-Fab policy. [E4; L1:8,169–174; design inference] |
| **RVT contact blending** | Caches shading data; supports world height and material attributes. Requires assets, a volume, writers and readers. [E6] | **Medium material/setup and page-pool/streaming cost.** Useful after relief exists: transfer floor color/normal near static rock/log contact, optionally gate with sampled height. RVT itself does not grow cushions or root geometry. Whole-island texel density must be designed: 3024 m / 16384 texels ≈ 18.5 cm/texel in a hypothetical square virtual page table; not a recommended setting or resident-memory estimate. Retain close detail from local materials. [E6; L2; calculation/design inference] |
| **Virtual Heightfield Mesh (VHM)** | Installed Epic experimental plugin renders a heightfield mesh from a virtual texture; component exposes an RVT reference, MinMax build data and LOD controls. [L9] | **High setup relative to this gap.** Separate RVT height/rendering path plus LOD/page management; collision and PCG alignment require lead verification. Not equivalent to RVT blending, and not a guaranteed Landscape replacement. Current editor/Metal/Lumen/shadow behavior is **unverified**. Lower priority than bounded mesh relief or one tessellation experiment. [L9,L6,L7; design inference] |
| **Projected decals** | Project base color/normal/roughness; cost scales with coverage and material complexity; receiving volumes can touch unintended objects. [E7] | **Low local setup, potentially high broad-screen cost when stacked.** Good for narrow litter seams, damp transitions and texture repetition. Cannot supply moss silhouette or cushion thickness. Use selective projectors after shape is right; not a carpet of overlapping decals as the terrain fix. [E7; V1,V2; design inference] |
| **UE 5.8 Mesh Terrain / Mesh Partition** | Experimental mesh-based terrain allows variable resolution, overhangs, and non-destructive modifiers. PCG has explicit partition query/write/projection tools; materials use partition channels, and RVT requires transformers. [E8–E10] | **High migration cost.** This is a real current alternative, not just a rumor or ordinary mesh scatter. Could support dense local floor geometry without uniform island refinement. But Landscape-only import/tracing and PNG sampling would need adaptation alongside material/PCG integration; there is no demonstrated need for caves/overhangs in this ground brief. Defer replacement of L_vilm. Mac support/performance for this workflow remains unverified. [L6,L7; E8–E10; design inference] |

## Ranked recommendation and bounded lead trials

1. **First: prove low relief in the existing interior.** At the fixed interior camera, arrange a small coherent group of installed mossy ground/boulder forms, partially embedded, around an existing root/log and into one open foreground patch. Preserve an appendable variant list. Tune the existing floor/coating together and inspect scale. Proposed trial forms: roughly 0.5–2 m across, 0.1–0.3 m visible rise; these are starting artistic guesses, not dimensions measured from the concept. Stop if they read as a rock garden. This tests the missing scale with the smallest dependency footprint. [V1–V3; L1:174,202–204; C2; L3,L4; design proposal]
2. **Second: bounded Nanite Landscape displacement comparison.** If the first trial confirms relief matters but the available forms cannot give a soft continuous floor, trial modest height-driven relief on the existing level, localized with a world-space mask; verify actual Nanite/build state and Metal behavior first. Compare the same view with displacement off/on and matched shading. Keep tree bases, water, paths and shore out until contact and scale work. Avoid converting the full island merely to run the trial. [E4,E5; L6,L7; design proposal]
3. **Third: repair visible contacts, then add local breakup.** If geometry achieves the right silhouette but joins remain obvious, compare matched material/embedding against RVT height/color blending at the same contact. Use projected decals for occasional seams/debris. Add the more complex infrastructure only if that comparison earns it. [E6,E7; V1,V3; design proposal]
4. **Fourth: alter base terrain only for the remaining broad-shape problem.** If a multi-metre ramp still dominates after low relief is visible, adjust a bounded interior macro-relief field with the existing pipeline and update its dependent height samples/placement. Do not reimport a uniformly finer island just to represent tiny moss. [L2,L6,L7; E1; design proposal]
5. **Defer VHM and Mesh Terrain migration.** Mesh Terrain is the strategic new UE5.8 option worth tracking; VHM is another experimental renderer, not an obvious upgrade path here. Neither has a verified project benefit sufficient to justify replacing working Landscape dependencies for this camera-level problem. Reconsider for a dedicated terrain-system requirement after the smaller trials. [E8–E10; L9; L6,L7; design recommendation]

Separate issue scopes: (a) relief shape and grounding, (b) floor material scale/coverage/contact, (c) any renderer experiment or terrain migration. This follows the requirement to split decision areas; research does not authorize worker visual editing. [AGENTS.md:14–17]

Lead acceptance: capture before/after fixed `interior` in **L_vilm**, using existing time controls, and compare to `interior_01`: raised soft moss forms between plants; almost no bare near litter; green continuous onto every visible flare; no floating/cut mesh margins. Assess blue/violet accents separately without expanding this terrain issue. Obtain another-family image review. Profile equal-resolution, equal-time views for GPU/material work, Nanite overdraw, VSM and streaming only after the shape comparison; no test map and no performance number substitutes for the visual targets.
