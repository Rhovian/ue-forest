# Glossary

**Forest Biome**: A reusable package that turns any landscape into forest: a PCG graph, the asset palette it places, and its placement rules. Not a UE Material.

**Asset Pack**: Source content as acquired from Fab (e.g. Megaplants: European Beech), before it is admitted to a Forest Biome.

**Asset Budget**: The written rules every asset must meet before a Forest Biome may place it.
